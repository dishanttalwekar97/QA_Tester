Assessment Protocol & Guidelines

⚠️ Strict Black-Box Testing Notice:

You will not be shown how many defects exist, nor will you receive completion percentages or hints. You must explore the application and identify defects independently based on software quality principles and the product requirements below.

How to Report Defects:

A floating "🐞 Report Bug" button is available in the bottom-right corner of every screen. Click it anytime to log an issue with:

  • Title / Summary: Clear, concise defect statement.
  • Steps to Reproduce: Unambiguous, numbered reproduction steps.
  • Expected vs Actual Result: Exactly what should happen according to spec vs what failed.
  • Severity: Accurate assessment (Blocker, Major, Medium, Minor).

Product Requirement Specifications (PRD):

Area Required Specification Behavior
Catalog & Search Search should handle text and alphanumeric input gracefully. Filter sliders must enforce valid ranges (Min ≤ Max). Product statuses (In Stock / Out of Stock) must accurately reflect warehouse state.
Cart Management Quantities must be positive integers ≥ 1. Quantities must not exceed available inventory. Changing quantity to 0 or removing items must cleanly update totals and line items.
Promotions & Shipping Promotional discounts (e.g. SAVE20) apply strictly once per order. Orders qualifying for free shipping ($50.00 threshold) must receive $0 shipping charge.
Checkout & Orders Form fields must accept valid punctuation (apostrophes, hyphens in names). Credit cards expiring in future years must be accepted. Order placement must prevent duplicate submissions on double-clicks.
UI & Usability All images must render or provide fallbacks. Modal dialogs should adhere to accessibility standards (e.g. keyboard navigation). Layout must remain fully accessible on mobile viewports.
